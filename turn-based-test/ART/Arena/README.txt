ARENA — a colosseum and its district
Top-down battlemaps for VTT and print. THET1ME.

WHAT IS INSIDE

Colosseum   50x50 squares. Upper level with the arena floor, the hypogeum
            below it inside the same walls, a labelled plan of the hypogeum
            for the GM, and a swept version of the arena with the market
            cleared away.
Barracks    44x44 squares. Ground floor with the training yard, upper floor,
            and the roof.
Villa       36x36 squares. Chambers and the roof.
Tavern      28x28 squares. Hall, rooms upstairs, and the roof.

Everything shares one scale and one palette, so the four buildings stand in
the same city.

HOURS AND STATES

morning, noon, evening, dusk, night, and night-combat (night graded dark
enough to fight in without losing the tokens).
winter, fire, ruin, ashes.

The geometry is cut once. An hour or a state changes the light and the
surface, never the walls, so you can swap sheets mid session and every token
keeps its square.

FILE NAMES

Name-view-state-hour-format.png

no suffix   32 px per square. Light file for a quick look or a small screen.
-VTT        128 px per square. Foundry, Roll20, Owlbear. No rescaling needed.
-grid-VTT   128 px per square with the grid baked into the image.
-print      shadows lifted and saturation pulled back, so paper does not turn
            the dark areas into one black blot.
-loop       eight seconds, seamless, webm and mp4. Fire, smoke, awnings, sand
            and cloud shadows move. The still frame of the loop matches the
            static map pixel for pixel.
